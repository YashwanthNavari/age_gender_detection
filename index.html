How It Works

🎯
Step 1 — Face Detection
A ResNet-10 SSD network scans the image at 300×300 resolution to detect all faces with confidence threshold >70%.
⚧️
Step 2 — Gender Classification
Each detected face region is passed through gender_net, a 227×227 CNN that classifies Male or Female.
🎂
Step 3 — Age Estimation
The same face region runs through age_net, predicting one of 8 discrete age buckets.
0–24–6 8–1215–20 25–3238–43 48–5360–100
